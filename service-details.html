Reliable test results, usually on the same day

Our on-site laboratory handles the tests most patients need, from routine blood counts to malaria and urine tests. Because samples are processed in the building, your doctor can review the results and start treatment during the same visit.

  • Blood tests, including full blood count and blood sugar.
  • Malaria, urine and stool tests.
  • Pregnancy and antenatal screening tests.

Some tests need preparation, such as fasting before a blood sugar test. Our staff will tell you what to do when you book, and our Test Preparation Guide lists the common ones.

Most routine results are ready within hours. A few specialised tests are sent to partner laboratories and can take up to 48 hours, and we will tell you the expected time before the sample is taken. Your doctor will always explain what the results mean and what to do next.
