    Our Services

    Complete healthcare for every age, from routine check-ups to specialist care

    General Care

    Check-ups, fevers, infections and long-term conditions such as diabetes and high blood pressure.

    Child Health

    Growth checks, childhood vaccines and gentle care for common childhood illnesses.

    Laboratory

    Blood, urine and malaria tests processed in our own lab, with most results the same day.

    Maternal Care

    Antenatal visits, scans and postnatal support to keep mother and baby healthy.

    Vaccination

    Routine and travel vaccines given by trained nurses, with records kept for you.

    Pharmacy

    Collect your prescription before you leave, with clear instructions on how to take it.
