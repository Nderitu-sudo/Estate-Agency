General Medicine

Everyday care for the whole family, from check-ups and infections to managing long-term conditions.

  1. Home
  2. Departments
  3. General Medicine

Complete care for everyday health

Our General Medicine team is your first stop for most health concerns. Whether you have a fever, a persistent cough or a question about your blood pressure, a doctor will see you, examine you and explain what is going on in plain language.

We also help patients manage long-term conditions such as diabetes, hypertension and asthma. Regular reviews, clear treatment plans and follow-up calls help you stay well between visits.

If you need a test, our on-site laboratory can usually return results the same day. If you need a specialist, we will refer you and share your records so you do not have to start from the beginning.
